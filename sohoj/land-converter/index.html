Standard measures used hereএখানে ব্যবহৃত মানদণ্ড

  • 1 decimal (shotok) = 435.6 sq ft = 1/100 acre
  • 1 katha = 720 sq ft ≈ 1.65 decimal
  • 1 bigha = 20 katha = 14,400 sq ft ≈ 33 decimal
  • 1 acre = 100 decimal = 3 bigha 8 chhotak (approx.)
  • Some districts use local bigha, kani or gonda sizes that differ. Check with the land office for your area.
