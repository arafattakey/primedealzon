The Best French Press Coffee Makers on Amazon

Glass or steel, big or small: three French presses worth buying, plus the 4-minute method that makes them shine.

A French press is the simplest way to make rich, full-bodied coffee. There's no paper filter, so more of the coffee's oils end up in your cup. There's also nothing electronic to break. You need coarse grounds, hot water and four minutes. The differences between presses come down to material, filter quality and size.

Best overall: Veken 34 oz French Press

The Veken is one of the most popular French presses on Amazon, and it gets the details right. The carafe is thick borosilicate glass, and the plunger uses a four-layer stainless steel filter (a spiral plate, a cross plate and two fine mesh screens) to keep sludge out of your cup. No plastic touches the coffee while it brews. It comes apart easily for cleaning, and the 34 oz size makes about four mugs.

How to make great French press coffee

  • Use a coarse grind, about 1 tablespoon of coffee per 4 oz of water (roughly 1:15).
  • Pour water just off the boil (about 200°F) over the grounds and stir gently.
  • Put the lid on and wait 4 minutes.
  • Press slowly and steadily, then pour everything out right away so it doesn't keep brewing and turn bitter.
