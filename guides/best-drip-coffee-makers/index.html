Quick tips for better drip coffee

  • Use about 2 tablespoons of ground coffee per 6 oz of water, then adjust to taste.
  • Use filtered water. Coffee is 98% water.
  • Descale every 1–3 months with a vinegar or descaler cycle.
  • Grind just before brewing if you can. It helps more than anything else.
