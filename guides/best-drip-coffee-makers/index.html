A good drip coffee maker should do three things well: get the water hot enough, spread it evenly over the grounds, and keep coffee warm without cooking it. Fancy displays come second. We narrowed Amazon's huge coffee aisle to four machines that do the basics right, each for a different kind of coffee drinker.

Quick tips for better drip coffee

  • Use about 2 tablespoons of ground coffee per 6 oz of water, then adjust to taste.
  • Use filtered water. Coffee is 98% water.
  • Descale every 1–3 months with a vinegar or descaler cycle.
  • Grind just before brewing if you can. It helps more than anything else.
