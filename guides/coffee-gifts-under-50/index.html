Coffee people are easy to shop for once you know what makes their morning better. The best gifts are things they'll use every day but probably wouldn't buy for themselves. Every pick below was under $50 on Amazon when we wrote this guide. Prices move a lot around Black Friday and the holidays, so check the current price before you order.

For the coffee lover who has everything: AeroPress Original

The AeroPress is the gift coffee enthusiasts recommend to each other. It combines French press, pour-over and espresso-style brewing in one small plastic press. You add coffee and water, wait about 30 seconds and press. Brewing and cleanup take about two minutes, and it's tough enough to throw in a bag for travel or camping. Even people who own fancy machines tend to keep one.

Bialetti invented the stovetop moka pot, and the Moka Express is still made in Italy. It makes strong, espresso-style coffee on the stove with no electricity and no pods. The 3-cup size makes about one mug's worth, which is perfect for one person. It's also a good-looking object on a stovetop, which helps for a gift.

Quick gift-buying tips

  • Match the gift to how they already brew. A drip-machine person may not want a manual brewer.
  • Add a bag of beans from a local roaster to make any of these feel more personal.
  • Order early. Popular coffee gear sells out in December, and shipping slows down.
  • Check that the item ships from Amazon or has free returns if you're not sure it's right.
