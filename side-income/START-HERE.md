# Week 1: start here (about 4 hours of your time)

The agents can't do these steps for you: each needs your identity, your bank account or your own judgement. Do them in this order. The first ones pay fastest.

## Day 1 (about 60 min)
1. **Profile:** copy `profile.example.md` to `private/profile.md` and fill it in (5 min).
2. **Research studies (#6):** sign up for [Prolific](https://www.prolific.com) and [User Interviews](https://www.userinterviews.com). Finish your profile fully, since that drives how many studies you're offered. Answer studies yourself; no AI or automation (20 min).
3. **Flipping (#5):** browse FB Marketplace "Free" and under-$20 listings within your pickup radius. Paste 5 candidates into `private/flip-inbox.md` and ask: "Use income-flip-pricer on my flip inbox" (20 min).
4. **Ledger:** copy `ledger.example.csv` to `private/ledger.csv` and delete the example rows (2 min).

## Day 2 (about 60 min)
5. **Freelance (#2):** "Use income-gig-hunter to write my Upwork profile and 3 Fiverr gigs." Review, then publish them yourself. Your Amazon reseller experience is the hook.
6. **Templates (#4):** open `products/reseller-profit-tracker.xlsx`, try it in Google Sheets, and tweak anything you'd change. Then "Use income-template-maker to write the Etsy and Gumroad listings for the reseller profit tracker." Upload it to Gumroad first (free), then Etsy.

## Day 3 (about 60 min)
7. **Websites (#1):** "Use income-site-builder to find 3 local businesses in [your city] with no website and build a demo for the best one." Review the demo, then send the one message it drafts.
8. **Newsletter (#3):** create a free [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com) or Substack. Publish one free teaser in the format of `examples/newsletter-issue-001.md`. Open the paid tier once 50 people are on the free list.

## Every week after (about 30 min plus doing the to-dos)
- Run **`/income-weekly`** in Claude Code. You'll get a scoreboard plus a short to-do list.
- Log every sale and cost: "bookkeeper: sold X for $Y on eBay, fees $Z".
- Check Prolific and User Interviews a few times a week.

## Optional (#7)
After 4 weeks, if you enjoy explaining things, start the YouTube channel: "Use income-video-producer to plan 4 tutorials." Record them in your own voice.

## When to quit something
At week 8, any business under 25% of its target gets dropped, and its hours go to the best one. Expect 1–2 of the 7 to be dropped. That's normal, and why the plan aims at $575 rather than $500.
